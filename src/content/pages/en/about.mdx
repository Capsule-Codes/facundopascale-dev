I'm Facundo Pascale. I write code, design architectures, break things, and teach other devs how not to break them. Founder of Capsule Codes, indie builder, mentor.

## My philosophy

**Concepts before code.** You can't build anything solid on shaky foundations. I'd rather spend a week understanding a problem before writing a single line than two months rewriting because I started wrong.

**AI is a tool — we are Tony Stark.** Jarvis executes, we direct. If you don't know what to ask the AI — or worse, you can't tell when the AI is lying — you're not using AI, it's using you.

**Against immediacy.** People want to learn React in two hours to land a job. They won't land that job. What they will get is starting over six months later when the next framework leaves them behind.

## What I do

- **Frontend architecture** — Angular, React, React Native, Tauri. Clean, Hexagonal, Screaming Architecture.
- **State management** — Redux, Zustand, Signals, GPX-Store.
- **Mentoring** — I help mid/senior devs escape tutorial hell and build their own technical judgment.
- **Capsule Codes** — my company, where we build custom software for founders and teams that need speed without sacrificing quality.
